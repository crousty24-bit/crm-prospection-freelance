## Brand & Style

This design system channels the rigor, mechanical precision, and prestige of elite international endurance racing (24 Hours of Le Mans, WEC, European Le Mans Series). Designed for gentleman drivers, corporate partners, race engineers, and passionate automotive collectors, the interface conveys technological mastery, high-velocity performance, and bespoke craftsmanship.

The visual style blends **High-Contrast Modern Editorial** with **Technical Precision**. It relies on architectural asymmetry, razor-sharp 1px structural framing, deep midnight backgrounds juxtaposed against technical light layers, monospaced indexing metadata, and refined typography. The experience feels like stepping into a factory prototype workshop: disciplined, aerodynamic, and commanding.

## Colors

The palette establishes high chromatic tension between endurance racing blues and high-contrast technical surfaces. 

- **Primary (`#1254A1`)**: The racing blue anchor used for signature interactive controls, primary CTAs, active highlights, and structural emphasis.
- **Secondary (`#091D35`)**: Deep midnight navy acting as the predominant structural tone for immersive dark canvases, hero backdrops, and navigation panels.
- **Tertiary (`#2E8CB8`)**: Aerodynamic cyan-blue derived from historic livery accents, used for secondary telemetry metrics, pill fills, and link hover states.
- **Neutral (`#657386`)**: Precision slate supporting tertiary typography, subtle 1px dividers, card stroke lines, and technical indices.

### Named Accent Colors
- **speed-crimson (`#E11D48`)**: High-urgency alert state and race-flag marker, reserved for live telemetry warnings, sold-out statuses, and French tricolor livery accents.
- **electric-cyan (`#00E5FF`)**: Pure luminescent touchpoint for live status pings, timing sector markers, and focal indicators.
- **light-surface (`#F4F7FA`)**: Crisp technical contrast section surface, allowing deliberate alternations between dark pitlane layouts and bright daylight editorial blocks.
- **carbon-black (`#040404`)**: Foundational deep backdrop for high-performance typography contrast.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is built on sharp speed, functional legibility, and technical precision:

- **Headlines & Counters (`Space Grotesk`)**: Geometric, modern, and slightly mechanical. Large scales utilize negative letter spacing to evoke tight aerodynamics and forward momentum.
- **Body & Captions (`Montserrat`)**: Clean, authoritative, and structurally balanced geometric sans ensuring effortless readability across long-form editorial narratives and technical service descriptions.
- **Technical Markers & Indices (`Space Mono`)**: Dedicated to racing telemetry, index chaptering (e.g., `/ 01 — TRACK`), lap times, and mechanical specs. Always styled uppercase with deliberate positive tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored by a 12-column architectural grid with generous breathing room. Asymmetric arrangements, split columns (e.g., 5-column editorial sticky headers versus 7-column media stacks), and vertical alignment lines mimic aerodynamic chassis blueprints.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Mobile` (< 768px): 4-column layout, edge margins of `1.25rem` (`margin`), cards stack vertically with `1rem` gap.
  - `Tablet` (768px - 1024px): 8-column layout, edge margins of `2rem`, gutters at `1.5rem`.
  - `Desktop` (> 1024px): 12-column layout capped at max 1440px canvas width, edge margins of `3.5rem` (`margin-desktop`), and gutters of `2rem` (`gutter-desktop`).
- **Section Transition Cadence**: Alternate between deep midnight navy (`#091D35` / `#040404`) and crisp light editorial bands (`#F4F7FA`) with explicit vertical padding (`space-xl` scaled 2x to 3x on desktop).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows; instead, it is articulated through **tonal layering**, **structural 1px hairline borders**, and **frosted cockpit translucency**:

- **Hairline Framing**: Standard UI containers use razor-sharp `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` on dark backgrounds, or `1px solid rgba(101, 115, 134, 0.2)` on light surfaces.
- **Frosted Surfaces (Cockpit Blur)**: Sticky headers and floating navigational docks use `rgba(9, 29, 53, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a bottom border of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Surface Elevation Tiers**:
  - `Layer 0 (Base Canvas)`: Deep carbon `#040404` or `#091D35`.
  - `Layer 1 (Card & Section Containers)`: `#0D2542` or `#122C4D` with 1px border.
  - `Layer 2 (Overlays & Dialogs)`: `#153359` with a subtle directional ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(18, 84, 161, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces an engineered, angular, low-radius shape profile (`roundedness: 1` — base `0.25rem` / 4px). This restraint reinforces rigidity, mechanical tolerances, and industrial automotive design.

- **Standard Containers, Cards, Inputs**: `rounded` (4px) to retain crisp silhouette edges without sharp razor corners.
- **Pill Badges & Live Status Tags**: Exceptionally styled as full pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to establish immediate visual differentiation from structural cards and layout tiles.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Action**: Racing Blue (`#1254A1`) background, pure white label in `Montserrat` Semibold, 4px corner radius, padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Features an inline right-facing arrow `→` that shifts +4px on hover.
- **Secondary / Ghost Action**: Transparent base framed by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, white text, shifting to white fill with dark navy text on hover.
- **Underlined Text Link**: Label with uppercase tracking (`label-uppercase`), paired with an accent cyan or crimson inline arrow.

### Pill Badges & Telemetry Chips
- **Category Badge**: Full pill border radius, subtle semi-translucent fill (`rgba(18, 84, 161, 0.15)`), `1px solid rgba(46, 140, 184, 0.4)`, uppercase mono typography (`index-marker` at 10px).
- **Status / Alert Badge**: Crimson pill (`#E11D48` background at 15% opacity, `1px solid #E11D48`), featuring a pulsing 6px colored dot indicator.

### High-Impact Photo & Program Cards
- **Media Containers**: Strict 16:9 or 4:5 aspect ratios framed by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Images feature subtle continuous zoom (`scale(1.04)`) transition over 500ms ease-out on container hover.
- **Content Overlay**: Heavy linear gradient scrim from bottom (`rgba(4, 4, 4, 0.95)` up to `transparent` at 60% height), elevating white headlines and date metadata badges.

### Statistic & Metric Counters
- **Metric Group**: Displayed in prominent grid tiers. The stat number is styled in `stat-counter` (`Space Grotesk` 56px bold) in white or racing blue, accompanied above or below by an `index-marker` or uppercase label in Slate (`#657386`) defining the achievement (e.g., `+20 YEARS COMPETITION`, `4 PARTICIPATIONS 24H DU MANS`).

### Form Controls & Text Inputs
- **Input Fields**: Minimalist dark slate fill (`#091D35`), `1px solid rgba(101, 115, 134, 0.3)`, 4px corner radius, white text, and a focused state driven by a high-contrast electric blue ring (`#1254A1`) with zero drop shadow.

### Sticky Header & Navigation
- **Header Bar**: Height `72px`, fixed sticky top, deep navy tint (`rgba(9, 29, 53, 0.85)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and bottom 1px separator line. Contains vector insignia, uppercase monospaced nav links, and a high-contrast primary contact CTA.

### Motorsport Heritage Footer
- **Footer Section**: Full-bleed Carbon Black (`#040404`) with architectural multi-column layout separated by 1px slate divider borders. Features technical location coordinates, Federation affiliations, legal mentions, and subtle watermark typography.
